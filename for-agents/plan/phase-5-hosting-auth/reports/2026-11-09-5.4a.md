## Asked
Only the Tech Success team may sign in; one superadmin adds and removes members from inside the app.
Also: the escalation DM header should read as a level reminder, not "Эскалаци — L2".

## Changed
- `prisma/schema.prisma` — `StaffMember { email @id, addedAt }`. `src/backend/db.ts` — `staffMembers` has/list/add/remove.
- `src/backend/auth.ts` — `ENTRA_ALLOWED_GROUP_ID` → `SUPERADMIN_EMAIL`; the session carries the lowercased
  `preferred_username`; `normalizeEmail`. No groups claim needed.
- `src/backend/server.ts` — membership checked at the callback (non-member: 403 page, no session) and on every `/api`
  request (removed member: 403, session cookie cleared); `/api/auth/me` adds `email`, `superadmin`;
  `GET/POST /api/members`, `DELETE /api/members/:email` (changes superadmin-only; superadmin cannot be removed;
  loopback dev without auth acts as admin).
- `frontend/src/features/teams/TeamsPage.tsx` — the "Баг" placeholder becomes the team list with add/remove for the
  superadmin; mn + en strings. Request helpers `fetchTeam`/`addMember`/`removeMember`.
- `src/reminder/reminder-message.ts` — escalation DM header `🚨 **<n>-р түвшний сануулга**` and a matching first line.
- Docs: `prd-hosting-auth.md` (req 3, 3a, env, out of scope), README runbook, `.env.example`, `env.md`.
